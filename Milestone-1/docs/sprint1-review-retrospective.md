# Sprint 1 — Review and Retrospective

**Sprint:** 1–10 October 2026 · [Issue #5](https://github.com/sa2305215/PearlMart-MileStone1/issues/5)

## Sprint Review
The team worked on PearlMart scope and initial system models, including the Context DFD, Level 1 DFD, Level 2 DFD and use case diagram. These deliverables underwent discussions and revisions; consult the corresponding issues and pull requests for dated evidence.

## Retrospective — What went well
Team members assigned responsibilities, communicated consistently and shared feedback through GitHub. Feedback led to revisions of the diagrams.

## Challenges
The DFDs required multiple revisions to maintain consistency and balance across levels. Some requirements were initially unclear, so the team consulted instructors repeatedly before finalizing modeling decisions.

## Improvement for Sprint 2
Continue consistent communication, discuss requirements as a group, confirm uncertain decisions with instructors, and cross-check related models. These were the team's stated intentions; their application in Sprint 2 should be documented separately in Issue #14.
