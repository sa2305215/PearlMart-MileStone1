# System Scope and Stakeholders

## In Scope

- Inventory records
- Supplier orders
- Receiving goods
- Sales processing
- Product replacements
- Customer requests
- Transaction logging
- Reports

## Out of Scope

- Supplier internal operations
- External financial accounting
- Payment provider processing
- Human resources management

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Administrator | Manages administrative information |
| Inventory Manager | Manages inventory and reports |
| Store Manager | Supervises receiving and storage |
| Salesperson | Processes sales and replacements |
| Supplier | Receives supplier orders |
| Customer | Receives receipts and requests replacements |

## Assumptions

- Users must log in before using protected functions.
- Inventory records are shared between warehouse and sales counters.
- Suppliers are external entities.
- Customer requests for unavailable items are recorded for follow-up.
